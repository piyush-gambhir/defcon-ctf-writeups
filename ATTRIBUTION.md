Original challenge authors and organizers retain any rights and attribution
stated inside their challenge packages. DEF CON and its marks belong to their
respective owners. This repository is an independent learning project and is
not affiliated with or endorsed by DEF CON.

Large challenge packages are not committed here. The fetch tool retrieves them
from the upstream archive, preserving the original directory layout. Write-ups
should credit the challenge author when known and link to the corresponding
upstream directory.
